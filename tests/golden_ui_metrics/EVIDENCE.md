Every expectation below is a recording row (source kind **rec**): the value in the recording is the value the test asserts. No expectation was invented or changed; no assertion is conditional on the result (a missing export skips the test with a reason). The rows were chosen by the sampling rule of `tools/gen_golden.py` (even spacing over the rows of the table, quota per table).
