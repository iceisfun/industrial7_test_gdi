## DPI states (pass 2 finding)
Repeated sweeps (2 each for ui_metrics and dpi_scaling, plus 2 cold144 boots) reproduce only at 96 DPI. At 120 DPI one of two repeats was clean;
144, 168, 192 and cold144 differed in every repeat. Moving cells: `GetStockObject(DEVICE_DEFAULT_FONT)` intermittently unreadable (co-varies with
`GetSystemMetricsForDpi(.., 72)` cells), measurement-DC LOGPIXELS (`dax/day`, `digx/digy`) taking 96, 120 or the stage DPI, one dc_default height
at 120, one header layout cell at 168. Stable: stages 144/168/192 of a logoff sweep report `desk_dpi=120`; cold boots report 96. Use data above 96
DPI as observations, not as a single reference; the golden areas therefore sample only 96 DPI and above only where the recording is stable.
